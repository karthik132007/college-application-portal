Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions, programs, and more

What is the eligibility criteria for admission? +

For UG programs: 10+2 with 50% marks in relevant subjects. For PG programs: Bachelor's degree with 50% marks. Entrance test may be required for specific programs.

Is there an application fee? +

Yes, there is a nominal application fee of ₹500 (non-refundable). Fee waivers are available for SC/ST/PWD categories as per government norms.

Can I apply to multiple programs? +

Yes, you can apply to multiple programs simultaneously. However, separate application fees apply for each program.

What documents are required for admission? +

10th & 12th mark sheets, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Aadhar Card, and passport size photographs.

Are scholarships available? +

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships up to 100% fee waiver. Additionally, government scholarships for SC/ST/OBC/Minority students are also available.

How do I contact the admissions office? +

You can call us at +91-XXXXXXXXXX (Mon-Sat, 9 AM - 5 PM) or email at admissions@aditya.edu.in. You can also visit our campus for in-person consultation.
